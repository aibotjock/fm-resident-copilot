What the audit found

High

Condition coverage remains incomplete

Seven original topic IDs receive structured regimens; the other 149 do not. Of 28 manually flagged treatment/prophylaxis/stewardship entries, 21 have no linked antibiotic lesson. This is a count of entries, not unique diseases or an exam-weighting estimate.

High

Retained historical teaching needs reconciliation

Priority examples: necrotizing-infection empiric coverage; legacy UTI duration/classification; AOM allergy/failure branches; cirrhosis/SBP diagnosis; Lyme age restrictions. Existing limitation text warns about several of these, but a warning is not a correction. Newly read sources corroborate the SSTI, cUTI and Lyme scope issues; the SBP error is also documented in the repository backlog and requires source-specific remediation.

High

Organ evidence remains incomplete

All 18 named drugs in antibiotic-tagged lessons have selective safety profiles. Four profiles have renal status not_established: penicillin V, azithromycin, fosfomycin and benzathine penicillin G. Seven have hepatic status not_established: amoxicillin, penicillin V, fosfomycin, levofloxacin, fidaxomicin, oral vancomycin and benzathine penicillin G. This is an evidence gap in the loaded extract, not proof that a dose reduction is medically required or that none is needed.

High

Population and route limits are material

Pediatric, pregnancy/breastfeeding, dialysis and hepatic-severity teaching remains selective. Strep pediatric dosing does not establish pediatric organ tables. Oral vancomycin CDI evidence cannot support IV vancomycin for sepsis, pneumonia or endocarditis. Some label/guideline differences are already disclosed, including nitrofurantoin kidney thresholds and CAP durations; preserve and resolve their scopes.

Medium

Supplemental condition integration is incomplete

No supplemental condition has a linked antibiotic regimen. Otitis externa explicitly lacks exact drops/dosing; endocarditis and rheumatic fever are diagnostic-only. Static code inspection shows ordinary guided topics call regimenInstructions(), while supplemental condition specialist instructions load criteria but not linked regimen instructions. Adding data mappings alone would not wire antibiotic teaching into those specialist condition lessons.

Medium

The existing audit does not measure the promised outcome

audit.mts inventories names, sources, profiles and lessons; it does not produce a per-condition completeness matrix. foundInRegimenLessons searches the JSON of all base lessons, so a safety/advanced-text mention can count as found. coverage.ts gives every unlinked topic complete_regimen_not_verified, including unrelated topics, and its SBP regex also matches systolic blood pressure.

Medium

Metadata is stale and counts can mislead

curriculum.json still says medication safety covers 11 named drugs across 13 existing regimen lessons, although actual totals are 23 safety profiles and 34 medication lessons. The 27 antibiotic-tagged lessons include antiprotozoal trichomoniasis/giardiasis uses; the 18-drug figure counts distinct named entities including a combination drug. Giardia has no topic_ids mapping and is standalone only. None of these counts proves breadth by condition.

Release gate

Do not mark expansion complete

No condition is demonstrated complete against the full agreed all-applicable-scenarios standard. This does not mean the stored regimens are all wrong: it means partial data, unresolved evidence and independent review limits prevent a completeness claim. No app data, main branch, deployment or assessment bank was changed by this audit.

What must be true before calling the expansion complete

  1. Every original and supplemental entry has an explicit relevance decision; every applicable syndrome is broken into population, severity, route, treatment versus prophylaxis, and supported alternative/escalation branches. A guideline need not define a universal first/second/third-line ladder.
  2. Each included branch has drug/formulation, units, route, interval and duration; weight-based doses state mg/kg/day versus mg/kg/dose and caps where supported. Maximum not applicable, not established and missing must remain distinct.
  3. Contraindication, avoid-use, precaution/monitoring and no-adjustment claims are differentiated and sourced.
